## Philosophy: Keep It Simple

Personal homepages have a bad habit of becoming over-engineered. This one doesn't. It's plain HTML, loads instantly, and says what matters:

- Who I am
- What I build
- How to find me

No build step. No JavaScript framework. No 500KB bundle for a page with three paragraphs. Just HTML delivered over GitHub's CDN.
